Word Chain: Voice First

Think fast. Chain words. Beat the clock.

A fast-paced word chain game where the last letter of each word starts the next one. Simple rules, endless challenge. Play by voice, even hands-free: the app says the letter out loud, and you just say your word.

What it does

🎤 Play hands-free

  • Say your word out loud instead of typing; the app submits it as soon as it recognizes it
  • Eyes-free play: the app announces the required letter, so you can play on a walk, at the gym or while doing chores

🎮 Game modes

  • vs AI: an adaptive opponent that gets harder as you improve, with Chain Goal, Sudden Death and No Timer options
  • Blitz: as many words as you can before time runs out, solo or pass-and-play
  • 2-Player: take turns on the same phone and outlast your opponent
  • Daily Challenge: a new themed category every day, like animals, cities or dinosaurs

🌍 Learn a language

  • 7 dictionary languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian and Swedish
  • A fun way to practice vocabulary and recall in another language (the game's menus stay in English)

🏆 Track your progress

  • Stats, streaks and game history
  • Chain Archive: revisit and share the full chain from any of your last 10 games
  • Game Center leaderboards and achievements

Free to download. No subscription: optional one-time upgrades remove ads, unlock all backgrounds, and add SAT and ESL vocabulary modes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play by voice?

Make sure Voice Input is on in Settings → Sound & Voice, then tap the mic button and say your word. The app submits it automatically once it recognizes the word. You can always type instead and press ✓ or Return.

How do I play without looking at the screen?

In Settings → Sound & Voice, turn on Voice Input and Announce Letter. The app then speaks the required letter, the game result and milestones out loud, and the mic starts automatically on your turn.

The app doesn't recognize my word. What can I do?

Speak clearly in a quiet place, and check that the dictionary language in Settings matches the language you're speaking; voice recognition follows the selected language automatically. If a word still isn't recognized, type it instead.

Why does the app need microphone and speech recognition access?

Only for voice input. It uses Apple's built-in speech recognition, the same technology as iPhone keyboard dictation, so speech is processed by Apple, not by us. If you prefer, turn Voice Input off and type your words.

How does scoring work?

Every valid word scores at least 1 point. Answering quickly adds up to 3 bonus points, and longer words score more: +1 for 6 or more letters, +2 for 8 or more. Faster and longer words are always better.

How do I beat the AI?

Open a 50-point lead over the AI. After you win, you can tap Keep Building to continue the chain with no score limit until the AI gets stuck.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.
