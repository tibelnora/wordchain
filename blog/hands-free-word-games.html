7 Hands-Free Word Games to Play Out Loud

November 2026 · 5 min read

No paper, no board, no screen. These word games need nothing but your voice, so you can play them on a walk, in the car, at the gym, while cooking or folding laundry. Most work with two or more people, and one of them you can also play alone with your phone.

1. Word Chain (the last-letter game)

2+ playersany age

The first player says a word. The next player says a word that starts with the last letter of that word: apple → elephant → tiger → river → rabbit… No repeats. If you can't think of a word in a few seconds, you're out.

Make it harder: only animals, only cities, or only words with 5+ letters. In Japan the same game is called shiritori.

2. Categories, A to Z

2+ playersany age

Pick a category (fruit, countries, movie titles) and go through the alphabet together: apricot, banana, cherry… Each player takes the next letter. Skip Q, X and Z if you're feeling kind.

3. Rhyme Chain

2+ playerskids love it

Someone says a word, and everyone takes turns adding a rhyme: cat, hat, bat, flat, chat… The first person who can't find a new rhyme starts the next round with a new word.

4. Twenty Questions

2+ playersany age

One player thinks of a thing; the others ask yes-or-no questions to guess it, with twenty questions at most. "Is it alive?" "Is it bigger than a car?" "Would I find it in a kitchen?"

5. The Story Game

2+ playersgreat for long drives

Build a story together, one sentence each. A fun variation: alternate sentences that start with "Fortunately…" and "Unfortunately…".

6. Word Association

2+ playersfast

Say the first word that comes to mind after the previous one: snow → winter → coat → pocket… No pauses, no explaining. Hesitate and you lose the round. It's surprisingly revealing.

7. Word Chain, solo, by voice

1 playeriPhone

Alone on a walk or doing chores? Let your phone be the other player. Word Chain: Voice First plays the last-letter game against you, out loud:

  • Turn on Voice Input and Announce Letter in Settings → Sound & Voice.
  • The app says the letter, the microphone opens by itself, and you just say your word.
  • The AI says its word out loud too, then gives you the next letter. You never have to look at the screen.
  • Play against the AI, try the Daily Challenge (a new category every day), or play 2-Player on one phone: the app announces whose turn it is.
  • Want to practice a language? Switch the dictionary to Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian or Swedish.

Voice recognition uses Apple's built-in speech recognition. Set the game up before you start; if you're driving, let a passenger handle the phone.

Why word games out loud are good for you

Quick word games make you search your memory under a little time pressure: recalling words, switching categories, thinking a step ahead. They're a light, fun way to keep your mind busy, practice vocabulary in a new language, and turn a boring walk or drive into something you look forward to.
