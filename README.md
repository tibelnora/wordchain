# Word Chain — Support                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                             
  Word Chain is a word game for iPhone. Every word must start with the last letter of the previous one.

  For support questions please use the contact form on the App Store listing.                                                  
   
  ---                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                             
  ## Privacy Policy

  Last updated: March 21, 2025                                                                                                 
   
  ### What data we collect                                                                                                     
  Word Chain does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.                                                         

  ### Voice Recognition
  Voice input is powered by Apple's built-in speech recognition — the same technology used for iPhone keyboard dictation. Audio
  is processed by Apple according to Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer never receives or stores your voice data.          
   
  ### Game Center                                                                                                              
  If you use Game Center, scores and achievements are handled by Apple. We do not have access to your Game Center data beyond
  what Apple provides through their standard APIs.                                                                             
   
  ### Advertising                                                                                                              
  The free version may show ads served by third-party networks. These networks may use cookies or device identifiers in      
  accordance with their own privacy policies.                                                                                  
   
  ### Contact                                                                                                                  
  For support please use the App Store review or contact option.
